# Project NANDA — Bangalore Chapter
> Regional hub for advancing decentralized AI-agent infrastructure in Bengaluru, India.

## Mission
Project NANDA (Network of AI Agents in Decentralized Architecture) builds foundational infrastructure for the Open Agentic Web — enabling trillions of autonomous AI agents to discover, collaborate, and transact securely across organizational boundaries.

Project NANDA develops open agent registries, cross-platform bridges (A2A, MCP), and verifiable trust primitives to empower a decentralized ecosystem.

## Key Facts & Stats
- Organization: Project NANDA (Network of AI Agents in Decentralized Architecture)
- Regional Chapter: Bangalore (Bengaluru, Karnataka, India)
- Global Stats: 10,000+ Community Members, 75+ Active Projects, 15+ Research Papers, 20+ Global Chapters
- Architecture: Decentralized, protocol-neutral, open-source (A2A, MCP, HTTPS)
